Academic Departments & Specialized Engineering Laboratories

Subramanian Polytechnic College offers 9 specialized 3-year diploma engineering programs in Rayavaram. Each department combines robust curriculum mastery with dedicated industrial workshops, CNC machinery, software suites, and 100% placement training.

Diploma in Computer Engineering (CSE)

3 Years (6 Semesters) Intake: 60 Seats DOTE Code: 1052 HOD: Prof. S. Muthusamy, M.E.

The Department of Computer Engineering delivers modern training across full-stack software development, operating systems, database management, and cloud infrastructures. Students gain daily coding experience in Python, C++, Java, web technologies, and Linux systems administration.

Advanced Laboratories
  • High-Speed LAN Computing Lab (60 Core i7 Workstations)
  • Python & Full-Stack Web Development Lab
  • Microprocessor, Hardware & IoT Sensor Workbench
  • Linux Open-Source Operating Systems Lab
Curriculum Competencies
  • Data Structures & Object-Oriented Programming
  • Relational Databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL)
  • Cloud Computing & Computer Networks
  • Cyber Security & Information Defense Essentials
Placements & Careers
  • Junior Software Developer / Web Engineer
  • Network Administrator / Cloud Support Tech
  • Recruiters: TI Metals, Lucas TVS, TCS, Wipro Core
  • 100% Campus Placement Assistance

Diploma in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML)

3 Years (6 Semesters) Intake: 60 Seats DOTE Code: 1055 HOD: Prof. P. Karthikeyan, M.Tech

SPTC's state-of-the-art AI & ML program trains students in foundational machine learning algorithms, deep neural network architectures, predictive modeling, and intelligent computer vision applications using high-performance GPU workstations.

Specialized AI Facilities
  • Dedicated GPU Deep Learning Computing Cluster
  • TensorFlow, PyTorch & Keras Modeling Suite
  • OpenCV Computer Vision & Image Processing Lab
  • Big Data Analytics & Statistical Tooling Bench
Key Core Modules
  • Supervised, Unsupervised & Reinforcement Learning
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) Fundamentals
  • Data Visualization & Exploratory Analysis
  • Applied AI in Industrial Automation & Robotics
High-Demand Careers
  • AI Assistant Engineer & ML Operations Associate
  • Data Analytics Technician & Prompt Engineer
  • Automation Testing & Robotics Associate
  • Direct pathway to B.E. / B.Tech Lateral Entry

Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (MECH)

3 Years (6 Semesters) Intake: 120 Seats DOTE Code: 1020 HOD: Prof. K. Rajasekaran, M.E.

One of the founding branches of SPTC Rayavaram since 1983. The department boasts extensive physical workshops spanning heavy machinery, CNC turn-mill centers, foundry, thermal engine testing setups, and advanced computer-aided design laboratories.

Machine Shops & Labs
  • Heavy Machine Shop (Lathes, Shapers, Slotters, Milling)
  • CNC Machining Center & G-Code Simulator Lab
  • Automobile & IC Engine Thermal Testing Lab
  • Foundry, Smithy, Sheet Metal & Arc/Gas Welding Shop
CAD/CAM Systems
  • Parametric 3D Modeling (Creo, SolidWorks, AutoCAD)
  • Strength of Materials & Structural Mechanics
  • Hydraulics, Pneumatics & Industrial Fluid Power
  • Metrology, Precision Gauging & Quality Control
Core Industry Recruiters
  • TI Metals & Tube Investments of India
  • Lucas TVS & TVS Mobility Group
  • Ashok Leyland & BHEL Ancillary Units
  • Over 40 Years of Mechanical Placement Track Record

Diploma in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)

3 Years (6 Semesters) Intake: 60 Seats DOTE Code: 1030 HOD: Prof. M. Sundaram, M.E.

The EEE department provides rigorous practical grounding in electrical machinery, industrial switchgear, power generation, PLC automation, and renewable solar power systems. Students acquire hands-on mastery of industrial motor drives and electrical safety standards.

Power & Drive Labs
  • AC & DC Electrical Machines Testing Laboratory
  • Power Electronics, Inverters & Motor Drives Lab
  • Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC) & SCADA Bench
  • Rooftop Solar PV Installation & Grid Telemetry
Practical Syllabus
  • Generation, Transmission & Switchgear Protection
  • Industrial Electrical Estimating & Panel Wiring
  • Renewable Clean Energy Systems & Energy Auditing
  • Microcontroller-Based Power Automation
Employment Avenues
  • TNEB / TANGEDCO Electrical Maintenance Technician
  • Solar Rooftop Project Engineer / Industrial Electrician
  • Schneider Electric, TVS, L&T Electrical Divisions
  • Electrical Contractor License (C-License Eligibility)

Diploma in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)

3 Years (6 Semesters) Intake: 60 Seats DOTE Code: 1040 HOD: Prof. V. Saravanan, M.E.

Specializing in microcontrollers, embedded IoT devices, RF wireless communications, PCB manufacturing, and signal processing. The department bridges digital hardware design with firmware programming for modern industrial instrumentation.

Electronics Labs
  • 8051, PIC & ARM Microcontroller Embedded Lab
  • Analog & Digital RF Wireless Communication Workbench
  • PCB Design, Etching, Soldering & Prototyping Workshop
  • Digital Signal Processing (DSP) & FPGA Simulation
Key Modules
  • Semiconductor Devices & Linear Integrated Circuits
  • Optical Fiber Telemetry & Satellite Systems
  • Consumer Electronics & Industrial Instrumentation
  • Embedded C & Sensor Interfacing
Career Paths
  • Embedded Firmware Associate / Testing Tech
  • Telecom Infrastructure Engineer (Jio, Airtel, BSNL)
  • Electronics Hardware Assembler / Quality Tester
  • Recruiters: Bharat Electronics, TVS, Foxconn

Diploma in Civil Engineering (CIVIL)

3 Years (6 Semesters) Intake: 60 Seats DOTE Code: 1010 HOD: Prof. A. Murugesan, M.E.

Equipping future civil supervisors and surveyors with practical competency in digital Total Station GPS land surveying, material testing, concrete technology, AutoCAD structural drafting, and sustainable building construction methods.

Field Surveying & Labs
  • Total Station & Digital GPS Geodetic Surveying Lab
  • Strength of Materials & Universal Testing Machine (UTM)
  • Concrete Slump, Compressive & Aggregate Testing Lab
  • Soil Mechanics, Geology & Environmental Hydraulics
Drafting & Management
  • AutoCAD 2D/3D Architectural Building Planning
  • Quantity Surveying, Estimation, Costing & Valuation
  • Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) Structural Detailing
  • Highway Engineering & Modern Construction Safety
Professional Careers
  • Site Engineer / Building Construction Supervisor
  • Licensed Surveyor & Quantity Estimator
  • PWD & Highway Department Contractor Assistant
  • L&T Construction, Sobha, Shriram Properties

  • Medical Imaging Systems (X-Ray, Ultrasound Physics)
  • Clinical Equipment Calibration & Safety Bench
Key Core Competencies
  • Bio-Sensors, Electrodes & Transducers
  • Diagnostic & Therapeutic Medical Devices
  • Hospital Equipment Management & NABH Standards
  • Biomedical Waste Safety & Radiation Hygiene
Hospital Placement Ties

  • Medical Device Field Applications Specialist
  • Hospitals: Apollo, Kauvery, Meenakshi Mission
  • GE Healthcare, Philips Medical, Siemens Healthineers
