About Subramanian Polytechnic College

A Legacy of Technical Excellence in Rayavaram

Established in the year 1983 by philanthropic trustees, SPTC has provided technical education and vocational skills to thousands of rural youth across Pudukkottai and surrounding districts.

Empowering Rural Youth with Future-Ready Technical Skills

Subramanian Polytechnic College is approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and operates under the Directorate of Technical Education (DOTE), Chennai. Situated in Rayavaram, Pudukkottai District, the college boasts high-end engineering workshops, computer labs, an Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC), and active student wings like NSS and Youth Red Cross.

MAM Palaniappa Chettiar Auditorium

Grand air-conditioned hall for national symposiums and convocation.

Selva Vinayagar Temple

Spiritual focal point within campus promoting peace and moral values.

Programs Offered

Computer Engineering

Software development, Python, Java, Database Management, Computer Networks, and Cloud Computing.

  • Full-Stack Web & Python Programming
  • Hardware & IoT Embedded Labs
  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats

Artificial Intelligence & ML

Machine learning models, neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, and data analytics.

  • GPU-Accelerated AI Laboratory
  • Python, TensorFlow & PyTorch Stacks
  • Predictive Modeling & Big Data
  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats

Biomedical Engineering

Medical instrumentation, physiological monitoring systems, healthcare telemetry, and diagnostic equipment.

  • Hospital Diagnostic & Imaging Lab
  • Biomedical Sensors & Transducers
  • Clinical Equipment Maintenance
  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats

Medical Laboratory Tech

Clinical biochemistry, hematology, medical microbiology, histopathology, and diagnostic laboratory operation.

  • Sterile Pathology & Culture Lab
  • Automated Hematology Analyzers
  • Clinical Biochemistry Reagents
  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats

Mechanical Engineering

Design, manufacturing, thermal engineering, CAD/CAM modeling, CNC programming, and industrial hydraulics.

  • Heavy Machine Shop with CNC Lathes
  • Automobile & Thermal Power Lab
  • Foundry & Welding Workshops
  • Annual Intake: 120 Seats

Electrical & Electronics

Power generation, electrical machines, switchgear protection, solar renewable energy, and industrial wiring.

  • AC/DC Electrical Machines Lab
  • Power Electronics & PLC Automation
  • Renewable Solar Installation Lab
  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats

Electronics & Communication

Semiconductor devices, microcontrollers, VLSI circuits, wireless RF telemetry, and digital signal processors.

  • Microprocessor & Embedded Lab
  • Digital Communication & DSP Kits
  • PCB Design & Fabrication Lab
  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats

Civil Engineering

Surveying, structural analysis, concrete testing, environmental engineering, and AutoCAD architectural drafting.

  • Annual Intake: 60 Seats
